Stumbles Creek @ Gravel Pit (203637)

Stream crossing - CBS

Barrier assessment

In-depth habitat investigation (data deficient structures only)

Embedment present for last 1/5 of outlet. Rearing habitat - good perennial flows, abundant cover, stable creek. Cover - overstream vegetation, undercut, and small woody debries. Spawning habitat - none - mixed cobble, gravel, fines. Overwintering habitat - fair - stream with approximately 3% gradient - fish would need to find low gradient locations to survive water velocities; fed by aquifer - consistent temperature throughout winter.

203642

Stumbles Creek @ Private (203642)

Stream crossing - CBS

Barrier assessment

In-depth habitat investigation (data deficient structures only)

Approximately 30m upstream form culvert channel splits into two branches. North branch flows through wetland carrying 20% of flow, South Branch follows road ditch carrying 80% of flow. Confluence of branches at UTM - 10.651476. 5560172. Spawning habitat - no spawning due to lack of suitable substrate. Overwintering habitat - downstream of culvert are steady enough flows for overwintering but upstream are reduced flows, and possibly are suitable only at higher flows; unkown flow regime above culvert. Suspected, but uncertain of aquifer flows. Channel is mis-mapped by TRIM - needs to be verified. Rearing habitat - stream provides good cover, but upstream N channel flows through wetlands (not preferable habitat for RB). South branch flows along the ditch for at least 50 meters - but unknown source - needs verification. Unknown fish habitat upstream of wetland - stream requires assessment to determine suitability for RB. Access to property not granted.

http://a100.gov.bc.ca/pub/pscismap/imageViewer.do?assessmentId=205315
